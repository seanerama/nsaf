# NSAF Story Preferences

Preferences for generating illustrated audio story ideas (consumed by the Story Maker pipeline).

## Themes
- friendship and finding common ground despite differences
- courage in the face of small fears
- empathy and listening before judging
- perseverance through setbacks
- kindness, generosity, and sharing
- honesty and earning trust
- curiosity and discovery
- belonging, home, and welcoming newcomers
- patience and waiting well
- gratitude and noticing small things
- forgiveness and second chances
- creativity and making things by hand

## Settings
- forest and woodland
- small village or town with a town square
- cozy home interiors at bedtime
- gardens, meadows, and orchards
- seaside, tide pools, and harbors
- weather events (storms, snow, fog, first rain)
- markets, fairs, and festivals
- farmhouses and barnyards
- attic and basement worlds full of forgotten objects
- a single tree or pond observed across seasons

## Character Types
- talking animals (mice, foxes, owls, badgers, rabbits, deer, raccoons, hedgehogs, crows)
- young children, ages 5-9
- gentle elderly mentors (grandparents, librarians, gardeners, lighthouse keepers)
- magical creatures (sprites, small dragons, kind ghosts, helpful clouds)
- anthropomorphized everyday objects (a teapot, a quilt, a single mitten)

## Exclusions
- adult themes
- on-page violence or scary monster imagery
- sad endings without resolution or comfort
- preachy or moralizing dialogue (let the action carry the lesson)
- branded or trademarked characters
- bathroom humor as the central premise
- death of a parent or pet as the inciting incident

## Target Age
- Min: 4
- Max: 8

## Length
- Min Minutes: 4
- Max Minutes: 10

## Art Style Defaults
- watercolor storybook
- soft pastels
- hand-drawn ink line with light wash
- cozy, warm palette

## Tone
- warm
- gentle
- a little playful
- quietly hopeful

## Model Profile
balanced

## Daily Quota
20
